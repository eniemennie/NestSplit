# NestSplit Demo Recording Guide

For the teammate recording the screen demo. Total length: about 2 minutes.

## 0. Setup (once)
1. Install Flutter and Git. Connect an Android phone with USB debugging on (or start an emulator).
2. `git clone https://github.com/eniemennie/NestSplit.git`
3. `cd NestSplit`
4. `flutter pub get`
5. `flutter run` (pick your phone if asked)
6. Tip: for a smooth recording use `flutter run --release`.

Demo account (tenant): `ben@nestsplit.test` / `Nest1234`

Before recording: turn on Do Not Disturb, charge above 50%, start your phone's screen recorder, restart the app so you begin on the splash.

> This is a prototype with sample data. Accounts and changes reset when the app restarts. Do not describe it as live with real users.

## Opening (about 10 s)
- Show the blue splash with the logo, then the login screen.
- Type the demo email and password slowly, tap **Sign in**.

## Step 1: See your household (about 30 s)
Screens: **Home**, then **More > Members**
1. Pause on the Home screen: greeting, "This week: X left to go", "Your chores today".
2. Point out the **Household share** percentage.
3. Tap **More** (bottom bar), open **Members**, show the roommates, go back.

## Step 2: Share the chores fairly (about 50 s)
Screens: **Chores**, a chore, **Home**, **Progress**
1. Tap **Chores** and scroll the list slowly.
2. Open one of your chores and tap **Mark as done**. Show the completed banner.
3. Go back to **Home**: the "left to go" count has dropped.
4. Tap **Progress**: pause on the **Fairness score**, "Chores done this week" and "Who did what".

## Step 3: Split the bill in seconds (about 40 s)
Screens: **More > Quick Split**
1. Tap **More**, then the **Quick Split** card ("Share a bill in seconds").
2. The title says "Electric bill". Enter an amount, for example `1200`.
3. Under **Who paid?** choose a roommate.
4. Show the **Who owes whom** list.
5. Tap **Add expense** and show the "Added: Electric bill" confirmation.

## Closing (about 10 s)
Open **Profile** (logo and tagline: Split the Bill, Share the Load), then stop recording.

## Optional: Landlord view (about 20 s, separate clip)
Profile > Log out. On the login screen tap **Landlord login**, then sign in as `landlord@nestsplit.test` (same password).

## Tips
- Record each step as its own clip and join them afterwards.
- Move slowly. Pause 1 to 2 seconds on each screen.
- If something looks wrong, restart the app and try again.
